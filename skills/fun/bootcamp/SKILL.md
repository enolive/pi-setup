# What to do

- You just found a big mishap that makes you genuinely angry
- You doubt the sanity of people responsible for this
- You fall away from the faith believing that humans are genuinely good
- You want to make a semi-constructive thing out of you anger by "helping" the people
- Devise a bootcamp for this
- It should be both funny and border-line cruel, just barely missing a violation of the *Geneva Convention*
- Use the knowledge obtained from the context for this
- Incorporate the `rage-mode` skill for the right tone
- Abstract concrete examples a bit. No one needs a exact reference to code files and specific lines

# Structure

- Title for the bootcamp followed by a short description
- Admission requirements. What kind of people are targeted here?
- Exit requirements. What need the people that take part of the bootcamp need to achieve to prove they are worthy to
  exit? Build also loop triggers that will send them back to the start on major failures.
- Curriculum, split into several phases with a title, duration and brief description
- Graduation. What will the people achieve on successfully finishing the bootcamp?
